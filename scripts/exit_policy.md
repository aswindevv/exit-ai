§1.1 Resignation notice period
Employees must submit written resignation with a minimum notice period of 30 days,
except during the probation period where 15 days' notice is sufficient. The last
working day is set by HR after manager approval and cannot be shortened without a
signed early-release waiver from the manager.

§1.2 Knowledge transfer (KT)
Every exiting employee must complete a knowledge-transfer plan with their manager,
covering ongoing projects, credentials to be handed over, and open action items. KT
sessions should be scheduled within the first week of the notice period and completed
at least 3 working days before the last working day.

§2.1 Exit interview
HR schedules an exit interview during the final week of employment. Attendance is
mandatory for all confirmed employees. The interview is confidential and its content
is used only for internal retention analysis, never shared with the employee's manager.

§3.1 Manager and KT sign-off
The reporting manager must review and approve the knowledge-transfer document before
the IT clearance stage can begin. An unapproved KT plan will delay the employee's
final clearance and settlement.

§4.1 Clearance sequence
An employee's exit clearance must be completed by HR, Finance, and IT before the final
relieving letter is issued. Clearances are processed in the order: HR resignation
acceptance, manager and KT sign-off, IT deprovisioning, then Finance settlement.

§4.2 Final settlement
Final settlement, including any pending salary, unused leave encashment, and
reimbursements, is processed within 45 days of the last working day, once all
clearances are complete. Settlement is paid to the bank account on file with HR.

§4.3 Provident fund and tax documents
Provident fund withdrawal or transfer requests are processed separately from the final
settlement and can take up to 60 days. Form 16 and other tax documents are issued
within the same financial year's filing window, typically by June.

§5.1 Company assets
All company assets, including laptops, monitors, mobile devices, and access cards,
must be returned to IT on or before the last working day. Unreturned assets are
deducted from the final settlement at replacement cost.

§5.2 IT deprovisioning
IT disables email, SSO, and VPN access at end-of-day on the last working day. Access to
shared drives and internal tools is revoked at the same time. Personal files on company
devices are not backed up and should be transferred out before the last working day.

§6.1 Relieving and experience letter
The relieving letter is issued once all three clearances (HR, IT, Finance) are marked
complete. The experience letter, confirming role, tenure, and last drawn salary, is
issued within 7 working days of the relieving letter.

§6.2 Rehire eligibility
Rehire eligibility is assessed internally by HR based on performance history and exit
conduct, and is not disclosed to the departing employee or their manager.

§7.1 Confidentiality after exit
Employees remain bound by the confidentiality and non-disclosure terms of their
employment contract after their last working day. This survives the termination of
employment and applies regardless of the reason for exit.
